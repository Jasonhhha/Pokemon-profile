The local GLB retains the source geometry, rig and embedded textures. Draco geometry was decoded for browser playback. The scene adds a relative lower-jaw morph on existing vertices, bone animation, materials and lighting.
The 24 roar poses and 12 breathing poses in kyurem-roar.webp were rendered locally from this same rig for the Canvas2D version.
